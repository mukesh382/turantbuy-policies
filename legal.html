Return Policy

▼

Return Window

  • Eligible items can be returned within 3 days of delivery
  • The return request must be raised from the app within these 3 days
  • After 3 days, the return option closes and returns are not accepted

Return Eligibility

  • The product must be unused and in its original packaging
  • All tags and seals must be intact
  • No signs of wear, damage or use

Non-Returnable Items

  • Grocery & food items: fruits, vegetables, dairy, bakery, meat, beverages, packaged foods, spices and snacks
  • Cosmetics and personal-care products once opened
  • Innerwear and other personal-use items, for hygiene reasons
  • Items damaged through misuse after delivery
Wrong, damaged, expired or missing items are always covered — even for non-returnable products. Report them from the order's Help screen within 24 hours of delivery and we will arrange a replacement or refund.

How to Return

  • Open My Orders → select the order → tap Help → Return this order
  • You'll be connected to our support team on WhatsApp with your order details
  • Tell us the reason for the return; once approved, we will arrange pick-up
  • Hand over the item unused, with all original packaging

Refunds

  • Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after the returned item is received and checked
  • Since orders are paid by cash on delivery, refunds are paid in cash or by UPI transfer — whichever you prefer

Delivery Policy

▼

Delivery Coverage

  • Every seller on TurantBuy delivers within their own service area
  • The app shows only the sellers and products that can be delivered to your selected address
  • If no seller covers your address yet, the app will tell you

Delivery Time

  • Each product shows the seller's expected delivery time before you order
  • Delivery times differ between sellers and may vary with traffic, weather, distance and item availability

Delivery Charges & Platform Fee

  • Delivery charge: set by each seller, and free once your order with that seller reaches their free-delivery amount
  • Platform fee: a small flat fee for each seller in your order
  • If you order from more than one seller, each seller's order has its own delivery charge and platform fee
  • All charges are shown before you confirm the order

Order Tracking

  • Live order status is available in My Orders
  • Orders move through: Placed → Accepted → Picked Up → Delivered
  • There are no separate SMS or email updates — please check the app

Delivery Address

  • Provide a complete address (house/flat number, street, landmark) and place the pin correctly on the map
  • The delivery partner may call you if the address is unclear

Failed Delivery

  • If the delivery partner cannot reach you, we will try to deliver again
  • If the order still cannot be delivered, our support team may cancel it
  • Since payment is taken only on delivery, an undelivered order costs you nothing

Cancellation Policy

▼

When You Can Cancel

  • You can cancel an order yourself while it is in the Placed stage — before a delivery partner is assigned
  • After a delivery partner is assigned, the cancel option is no longer shown. Contact support as soon as possible and we will try to help
  • Once an order has been picked up for delivery, it cannot be cancelled

How to Cancel

  • Open My Orders and select the order
  • Tap Help → Cancel my order and confirm

Refund After Cancellation

  • All orders are cash on delivery, so nothing is paid in advance
  • Cancelling before delivery costs you nothing — there is no amount to refund

Cancellation by TurantBuy or the Seller

  • An order may be cancelled if the item goes out of stock, the seller is unavailable, or the order cannot be delivered
  • You will see the cancellation in My Orders, and no charge applies

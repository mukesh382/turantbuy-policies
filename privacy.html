Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run your account and deliver your orders:

  • Your name, gender and mobile number
  • Delivery addresses you save, including the location you pick on the map
  • Your current location, only while you are choosing a delivery address on the map
  • Your orders and order history
  • Reviews and ratings you post
  • A device identifier, used to keep your account signed in on one phone at a time

We do not ask for your email address, and we do not collect photos, contacts or other files from your phone.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To place, prepare and deliver your orders
  • To show you the sellers and products available at your delivery address
  • To help the delivery partner reach you
  • To answer your questions and handle complaints
  • To keep your account secure and prevent misuse

We do not use your data to show advertisements, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to anyone.

The app relies on these services, which process data on our behalf under their own data-protection terms:

Google Firebase Authentication
Used for: verifying your mobile number with a one-time code (OTP login)
Data: your mobile number

Google Maps

Voice search uses your phone's own speech-recognition service (usually Google), and only when you tap the microphone icon. TurantBuy receives just the text of what you said.

4. Information Sharing

We share only what is needed, and only with:

  • The seller fulfilling your order — the items you ordered and your delivery details
  • The delivery partner — your name, delivery address and mobile number
  • Other users — your name appears next to reviews you post
  • Authorities — only when required by law

5. Data Security

  • All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Our servers and database are protected by Cloudflare
  • Login uses a one-time code sent to your mobile number — there is no password to leak
  • Only authorised staff can access customer data, and only for support work

6. Location Data

We ask for location permission so you can quickly pick your delivery address on the map. We then use your saved delivery address to find the sellers who can deliver to you.

  • Location is used only while the app is open — never in the background
  • You can refuse or turn off location permission at any time from your phone's settings
  • Without it, you can still choose your address by moving the map yourself

7. Device Permissions

TurantBuy asks for only one permission:

  • Location — to help you pick your delivery address. It's optional; the app works without it.

The app does not request access to your camera, photos, contacts, microphone or storage.

8. Your Rights

You can, at any time:

  • See and correct the details saved in your profile
  • Delete your account and the personal data linked to it
  • Ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Withdraw your consent for us to process your data

To delete your account, open the app and go to Profile → Delete account. You can also write to us at help.turantbuy@gmail.com. See our account deletion page for details.

9. Data Retention

  • Your profile and saved addresses are deleted when you delete your account
  • Order and billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, with your name, mobile number, address and location removed
  • Your reviews remain on the products but are shown as "Deleted user"
  • We do not keep data longer than needed

10. Children's Privacy

TurantBuy is meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

11. Payment

TurantBuy orders are paid by cash on delivery. You pay the delivery partner when your order arrives, so the app never handles card numbers, UPI PINs or bank details, and no payment data is stored.

12. Where Your Data Is Stored

Our servers are run on Cloudflare, and mobile-number verification and maps are provided by Google. These are global services, so your data may be processed on servers outside India under their standard data-protection safeguards. By using TurantBuy you consent to this.

13. Third-Party Links

The app may link to outside websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices — please read their policies before sharing anything.

14. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. Important changes will be shown in the app. Continuing to use TurantBuy means you accept the updated policy.

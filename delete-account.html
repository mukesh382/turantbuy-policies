How to delete your TurantBuy account

You can delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time. There are two ways.

From inside the app (instant)

  1. Open the TurantBuy app and go to Profile
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account
  3. Enter the verification code we send to your registered mobile number
  4. Choose a reason for leaving (you can also add a note)
  5. Tap Delete permanently

Your account is deleted immediately.

You can't delete your account while an order is still in progress. Wait until it is delivered or cancelled, then try again.

Without the app

Send us an email or a WhatsApp message from your registered mobile number with:

  • Your registered mobile number
  • The words "Delete my TurantBuy account"

We complete these requests within 7 working days, and may call your registered number once to confirm the request is genuine.

What is deleted

We permanently delete:

  • Your name, gender and registered mobile number
  • All your saved delivery addresses and their map locations
  • Your account and login record

What is kept

Past order and billing records are kept for up to 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting law, so sellers can keep their sales records. Before keeping them, we remove your name, mobile number, delivery address and location, so they can no longer be linked back to you.
  • Reviews you wrote stay on the products but show "Deleted user" instead of your name
  • The reason you chose for leaving is kept anonymously to help us improve

Please note

Deletion cannot be undone. Your order history will no longer be visible to you, and you will need to register again to place a new order.

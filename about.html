Who we are

TurantBuy brings the shops in your own neighbourhood to your phone. From groceries and vegetables to fashion, beauty products, food and books — what your local shops sell, you can order right here.

Your order doesn't come from a distant warehouse. It is packed by a shop in your area and delivered to your door.

Our mission

To save you the trip to the market. The same shops you already trust — without the queues or the walk in the sun. Just a few taps.

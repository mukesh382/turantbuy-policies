Why do the products change when I change my address?

Turant Buy only shows what sellers near your delivery address can actually deliver to you. When you change the address, we check which sellers cover it and reload their products, prices and stock.

Why is an item showing as unavailable?

Stock belongs to one specific seller. An item becomes unavailable if that seller runs out, goes offline, or no longer covers your delivery address. It stays in your cart so you do not lose it - remove or replace it to continue to checkout.

I ordered from more than one seller. Why did I get several order IDs?

Each seller packs and delivers their own items, so every seller gets a separate order with its own order ID, delivery fee and delivery code. You can track and cancel each one on its own.

How are delivery and platform fees calculated?

Delivery is charged per seller at that seller rate, and becomes free once your subtotal with that seller crosses their free-delivery limit. The platform fee is a flat charge per seller in your order.

What is the delivery code?

Every order shows a code in the app. Share it with the delivery partner only when your items are in your hands - it is what confirms the delivery was completed.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes. Open My orders, select the order and choose Help, any time before it is delivered. The stock goes straight back to the seller. Once an order is delivered it can no longer be cancelled.

The amount changed between my cart and the payment screen. Why?

Prices and stock are confirmed with the seller once more just before payment. If anything changed in the meantime, we show you the new amount first - you are never charged on a stale price.

How do I add or change my delivery address?

Tap your address at the top of the home screen, then pick a saved address or add a new one. You can drop a pin on the map so the delivery partner finds you easily.

Do you deliver to my area?

Add your address and the app tells you right away. If no seller covers it yet, the home screen will say so and you can try another address.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and choose Delete account at the bottom. We send a code to your registered number to confirm it is you. Your name, phone number and saved addresses are deleted permanently. Past orders stay with the sellers for their records but are no longer linked to you. You cannot delete your account while an order is still in progress.

I need help with one specific order.

Open My orders, select that order and choose Help. The options you see depend on where the order has reached.

Help & Support

Why do the products change when I change my address?

TurantBuy only shows what sellers near your delivery address can actually deliver to you. When you change your address, we check which sellers cover it and reload their products, prices and stock.

Why is an item showing as unavailable?

Stock belongs to one specific seller. An item becomes unavailable if that seller runs out, goes offline or closes for the day, or no longer covers your delivery address. It stays in your cart so you don't lose it — remove or replace it to continue to checkout.

I ordered from more than one seller. Why did I get several order IDs?

Each seller packs and delivers their own items, so every seller gets a separate order with its own order ID, delivery fee and delivery code. You can track and cancel each one separately.

How are delivery and platform fees calculated?

Delivery is charged per seller at that seller's rate, and becomes free once your subtotal with that seller reaches their free-delivery limit. The platform fee is a small flat charge for each seller in your order. All fees are shown before you place the order.

What is the delivery code?

Every order shows a code in the app. Share it with the delivery partner only once your items are in your hands — it confirms the delivery was completed.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, while the order is still in the "Placed" stage — before a delivery partner is assigned. Open My orders, select the order, choose Help and tap Cancel my order. The stock goes straight back to the seller. After a delivery partner is assigned, contact support and we'll try to help.

The amount changed before I placed my order. Why?

Prices and stock are confirmed with the seller once more just before your order is placed. If anything changed in the meantime, we show you the new amount first — you are never charged an outdated price.

How do I pay?

Orders are paid by cash on delivery. You don't pay anything in advance — pay the delivery partner when your order arrives.

How do I add or change my delivery address?

Tap your address at the top of the home screen, then pick a saved address or add a new one. You can drop a pin on the map so the delivery partner finds you easily.

Do you deliver to my area?

Add your address and the app tells you right away. If no seller covers it yet, the home screen will say so and you can try another address.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and choose Delete account at the bottom. We send a code to your registered number to confirm it's you. Your name, phone number and saved addresses are deleted permanently. Past orders stay with the sellers for their records but are no longer linked to you. You can't delete your account while an order is still in progress.

I need help with a specific order.

Open My orders, select that order and choose Help. The options you see depend on how far the order has progressed.
